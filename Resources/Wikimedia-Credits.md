# Wikimedia Commons credits

KeyFlag's bundled flags (`Resources/Flags`) and coats of arms (`Resources/CoatsOfArms`) are PNG renders of these Wikimedia Commons files, scaled down. Each keeps its own license, shown below; they are not covered by KeyFlag's MIT license. Arms marked ✂ show only the shield, cut out of the full achievement. Regenerate with `Scripts/fetch-wikimedia-art.py`.
